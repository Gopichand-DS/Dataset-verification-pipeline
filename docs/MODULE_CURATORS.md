
# Module Curators

AIOrbit now has explicit module schemas and domain-specific evidence requirements.

## Tools
Verify that the product itself exists and that its capability/use case is
supported by the official site. Directory presence alone is insufficient.

## Companies
Verify that AI is central/meaningful to the company rather than merely an
internal capability or a superficial feature. Funding, valuation and employee
counts require their own authoritative evidence.

## Agents
Verify genuine agentic behavior: planning/execution of multi-step work, tool or
application interaction, monitoring/action, or another documented autonomous or
semi-autonomous workflow. A chatbot alone is not sufficient.

## MCP
Verify that the candidate is actually an MCP server/client and capture the
specific capabilities/integrations from documentation or the official project.

## Robots
Verify a real system or sufficiently evidenced research system. Status must be
distinguished from concept/prototype/research/pilot/pre-order/commercial/
deployed.

## Devices
Verify that AI is meaningful to the physical product, not merely marketing
language. Model/generation and availability should be separately evidenced.

## Models
Verify model identity at the family/base-model level and avoid counting
provider-hosted versions or quantizations as separate underlying models.

## News
Treat an article as an event candidate. Group articles describing the same
underlying event and retain the strongest authoritative source.
